    Getting started

    You have two passwords: the account password signs you in, the master password decrypts your data. The master password never leaves your device and can never be recovered - keep it written somewhere safe. Day to day you'll unlock with your 6-digit PIN instead.

    Filling logins

    Open a site's login page, click OptiPass, and press the ⬇ button on the matching entry - username and password fill in, and if the entry has a 2FA key its current code is copied so you can paste it when asked.

    2FA one-time passwords

    When a site offers authenticator-app 2FA, choose "enter code manually" and paste the setup key into the entry's 2FA field. OptiPass then generates the rotating 6-digit code - same as Google Authenticator, shared with your vault's members. Keep recovery codes in the Notes.

    Vaults are your teams

    Personal is cryptographically private - not even admins can read it. Shared vaults (e.g. AI Team, RoosterPartner) are visible only to the members an admin adds; passwords never cross between teams.

    Credit & usage monitors

    Edit an entry and add metrics (Credits, Bandwidth...) read from the tool's dashboard page or its API. Readings show in a box under the entry, turn red below your threshold, and a red badge appears on the OptiPass icon. API keys for monitors are stored end-to-end encrypted.

    FAQ

    Forgot your master password? It cannot be recovered. An admin resets your keys (SETUP.md explains how); your personal vault is lost but shared vaults come back when a manager re-adds you.

    Forgot your PIN? Click "Use master password instead" - after 5 wrong PIN tries it resets automatically.

    New teammate? Admin screen > Invite - send them the invite code; they install OptiPass, sign up with the code, and set their own passwords.

    Updating? Updates apply automatically. To force one: Settings > Check for updates.
