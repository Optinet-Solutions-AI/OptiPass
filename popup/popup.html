    Getting started

    You have two passwords: the account password signs you in, the master password decrypts your data. The master password never leaves your device and can never be recovered - keep it written somewhere safe. Day to day you'll unlock with your 6-digit PIN instead.

    Filling logins

    Open a site's login page, click OptiPass, and press Fill login on the matching tool - username and password fill in, and if the tool has a 2FA key its current code is copied so you can paste it when asked.

    2FA one-time passwords

    When a site offers authenticator-app 2FA, choose "enter code manually" and paste the setup key into the tool's 2FA field. OptiPass then generates the rotating 6-digit code - same as Google Authenticator, shared with your vault's members. Keep recovery codes in the Notes.

    Vaults are your teams

    Personal is cryptographically private - not even admins can read it. Team vaults (e.g. AI Team, RoosterPartner) are visible only to the members a vault manager adds; passwords never cross between teams.

    Credit & usage monitors

    Edit a tool and add metrics (Credits, Bandwidth...) read from the tool's dashboard page or its API. Readings show in a box under the tool, turn red below your threshold, and a red badge appears on the OptiPass icon. API keys for monitors are stored end-to-end encrypted.

    FAQ

    Forgot your account password? On the sign-in screen click "Forgot account password?" and we email you a link. It opens the OptiPass website, where you choose a new account password. Your master password and your tools stay the same.

    Forgot your master password? It cannot be recovered. An admin resets your keys (SETUP.md explains how); your Personal vault is lost but team vaults come back when a vault manager re-adds you.

    Forgot your PIN? Click "Use master password instead" - after 5 wrong PIN tries it resets automatically and you're offered a new one.

    New teammate? Admin screen > Invite by email copies a signup link - send it to them. It opens OptiPass with their invite and email filled in, and they choose their own passwords.

    Updating? Installed from the Chrome Web Store: Chrome updates OptiPass by itself. Installed from the zip or with git: when the list says "OptiPass vX is available", click How to update (Settings > Updates has the steps).
